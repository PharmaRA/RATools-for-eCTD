# EU M1 source snapshot

This directory contains a dated snapshot of the EU eCTD Module 1 materials
published by the European Medicines Agency (EMA) and its eSubmission Expert
Group. The authoritative source page is recorded in `manifest.json`; every
bundled file has a SHA-256 digest and the DTD has the published MD5 digest.

The snapshot is acquired for traceability and implementation. It is not
licensed under the repository's AGPL-3.0 license. It is redistributed under
EMA's [copyright and limited reproduction notice][ema-legal], which permits
reproduction and distribution of public EMA information and documents for
commercial and non-commercial purposes when EMA is acknowledged as the source.
That permission does not apply to third-party content.

Attribution: European Medicines Agency (EMA), EU eCTD Module 1 materials,
accessed August 2026. The files are retained byte-for-byte; their hashes are
recorded in `manifest.json`.

The production EU profile remains a controlled skeleton. Do not describe EU
publishing as formally compliant until the writer, Section Dictionary,
validation rules, lifecycle handling, and certification fixtures have been
updated against this snapshot.
