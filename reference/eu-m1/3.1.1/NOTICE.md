# EU M1 source snapshot

This directory contains a dated snapshot of the EU eCTD Module 1 materials
published by the European Medicines Agency (EMA) and its eSubmission Expert
Group. The authoritative source page is recorded in `manifest.json`; every
bundled file has a SHA-256 digest and the DTD has the published MD5 digest.

The snapshot is acquired for traceability and implementation. It is not a
claim that the source materials are licensed under the repository's AGPL-3.0
license. Review the EMA copyright/disclaimer and the project's redistribution
approval before shipping these files outside the development repository.

The production EU profile remains a controlled skeleton. Do not describe EU
publishing as formally compliant until the writer, Section Dictionary,
validation rules, lifecycle handling, and certification fixtures have been
updated against this snapshot.
